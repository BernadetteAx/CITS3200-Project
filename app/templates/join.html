HOW TO PLAY
CHALLENGE CATASTROPHE · CO-OP SURVIVAL
1 Each round, the item shop presents a pair of items. Vote together as a team to buy the ones you'll need.
2 Your crew is dropped into a randomly-generated Mission which is a run of Challenges you must get through.
3 Each Challenge can be solved by one of a few possible items. Got the right one? Clean solve. Missing it? You'll find the long way round... it works, just costs you points.
4 There's no losing outright just how chaotic your run gets. Everyone wins or loses together.
